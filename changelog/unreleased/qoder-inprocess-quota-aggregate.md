### English

- Qoder PAT accounts: the console quota headline now sums the plan quota, add-on pack, and organization resource package, matching the official client's usage view. Previously only the plan bucket was shown, so an account living on its add-on balance (e.g. after the plan ran out) displayed 0/0.

### 中文

- Qoder PAT 账号：控制台额度总览现在会把套餐内额度、资源包与组织资源包三个桶求和显示，与官方客户端的用量页一致。此前只显示套餐内额度，套餐耗尽后依赖资源包的账号会错误显示为 0/0。
