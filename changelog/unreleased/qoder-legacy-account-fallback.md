### English

- Legacy Qoder accounts added before v0.7.0 (CLI login, no PAT) fall back to the worker again, so check-in, credit refresh and model catalog refresh no longer fail with "account not found".

### 中文

- v0.7.0 之前通过 CLI 登录添加、尚无 PAT 的 legacy Qoder 账号重新回退到 worker，签到、积分刷新和模型目录刷新不再报 "account not found"。
