### English

- **Console** · Overview rankings drill down: clicking a model, account or error kind opens the logs page filtered to it over the overview's current window, and drag-selecting a range on the traffic chart opens the logs for that range. The logs page filters now live in the URL and stay in sync with its controls.
- **Console** · The overview time window adds 30d and a custom from/to range. Stats refresh every 30s in the background (no skeleton), with a pause toggle, and skip refreshes while the tab is hidden.

### 中文

- **界面** · 概览排行支持下钻：点击模型、账号或错误类型，会按概览当前时间窗口打开已筛选的日志页；在流量图上拖选时间段，可直接查看该时段的日志。日志页筛选条件写入 URL，并与筛选控件双向同步。
- **界面** · 概览时间窗口新增 30 天与自定义起止时间；统计每 30 秒在后台刷新（不显示骨架屏），可暂停，标签页隐藏时不刷新。
